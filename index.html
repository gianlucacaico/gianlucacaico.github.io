Spotlight

Dynamic blueprinting

One capability inside the EXD model

A service blueprint is usually accurate on the day it's signed off and out of date the day after. Dynamic blueprinting treats it as software instead: all evidence lands in one ledger, and the blueprint is simply a view over it. Here's the framework in four steps, using an everyday example: returning an online order.

Capture every input in one place

Interviews, site visits, data and workshops all land in the same ledger before anything is mapped. Nothing lives only in someone's notebook.

Customer interviewsStore visit notesReturns dataStaff workshop
↓
One ledgerEvery input goes through the same door

Break it into sourced claims

Each input becomes atomic claims: one statement per row, typed as fact or hypothesis, with a source and a date. That's what makes a finding defensible months later.

Most customers start a return on their phoneFactReturns data
Customers find the returns label confusingHypothesisStaff workshop
Store staff re-scan every returned parcelFactStore visit notes

Flag contradictions, don't smooth them

When two claims disagree, both stay on the board with their sources attached. Often both are true and the real question is a definition, which is far cheaper to settle in discovery than in delivery.

Refunds are issued within 3 daysFactStaff workshop Flagged
Customers wait 10+ days for their moneyFactCustomer interviews Flagged

Both are true: one clock starts at the warehouse, the other at drop-off. One definition gets agreed and becomes the baseline.

Render the blueprints as views

The current and target state blueprints are views over the ledger. When new evidence lands, the views re-run and every step keeps its link to the claims behind it. Try adding a late input.

Illustrative example.

Evidence in days

Synthesis runs continuously, so findings arrive during discovery rather than in a block at the end of it.

Findings that outlast the room

Every claim keeps its lineage, so decisions survive challenge, sponsor changes and new delivery partners.

A target state in delivery units

Steps are marked proposed or retired against the step they replace: a build list and a stop-doing list, not a report.

A number without lineage is an opinion. An insight without a traceable source is the same thing in a nicer font.
